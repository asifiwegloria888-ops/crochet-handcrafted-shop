Find your size

Every piece is made to measure. These measurements help you to choose between Small, Medium, Large and XL.

The measurements below are a guide, in centimeters.
SizeBustWaistHips
Small84 - 88 cm64 - 68 cm90 - 94 cm
Medium92 - 96 cm72 - 76 cm98 - 102 cm
Large100 - 104 cm80 - 84 cm106 - 110 cm
XL108 - 112 cm88 - 92 cm114 - 118 cm

Take your measurements

  • BustAround the fullest part of your chest, with the tape flat.
  • WaistAround the narrowest part of your body, above the belly button.
  • HipsAround the widest part of your hips and bottom.

Between two sizes, or a very special body shape? No problem: write your measurements in the box "Anything else?" when you order, and I make your piece to measure.
